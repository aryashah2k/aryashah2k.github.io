Essays on four papers.

  1. Jun 2026 Sycophancy as a Multilingual Alignment Failure: How Safety Degrades Across Languages, Topics, and Models Shah, Beniwal, Singh, and Silpasuwanchai measure sycophancy across 38 languages and find higher rates in low-resource and zero-shot tiers.
  2. Apr 2026 Too Nice to Tell the Truth: Quantifying Agreeableness-Driven Sycophancy in Role-Playing Language Models Shah, Mishra, and Silpasuwanchai test whether a persona's agreeableness predicts how often a role-playing model endorses the user.
  3. Nov 2025 In Search of Goodness: Large Scale Benchmarking of Goodness Functions for the Forward-Forward Algorithm Shah and Tripathi compare 21 goodness functions for the Forward-Forward algorithm on four image datasets.
  4. Nov 2025 Purrturbed but Stable: Human-Cat Invariant Representations Across CNNs, ViTs and Self-Supervised ViTs CVPR 2026. Shah and Tripathi compare frozen vision encoders on paired human frames and cat-vision-filtered frames.
