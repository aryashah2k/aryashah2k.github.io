About Me

I am a second-year M.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Gandhinagar. I am also pursuing an M.S. in Data Science and AI from AIT Thailand.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision. I am passionate about developing AI systems that can understand and generate human-like responses, as well as applications in healthcare and environmental sustainability.

Previously, I worked as an AI Engineer Intern at Think Blue Data on a UN ESCAP project, and as an Associate Software Engineer at ZS Associates. I have published research in JCRT, Springer, and IEEE Xplore.
